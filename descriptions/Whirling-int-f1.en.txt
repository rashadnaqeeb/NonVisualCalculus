zone.tables.title = the dining hall
zone.tables.body = The front of the café, where the plaza door opens in. Three long communal tables with benches run across a floor of chipped green and white tiles, between square brick pillars fitted with strip lights. Glass partitions hung with trailing ivy wall off the window seats along the front, where small round tables and red chairs stand under the tall panes. Pot plants crowd the corner by the door.
zone.bar.title = the bar
zone.bar.body = A curved counter of green-painted wood and brass rail, with four red-topped stools along its front and a canopy of pipes, glasses and bottle shelves above it. The floor in front is a sunburst mosaic: an eight-pointed star of white and gold tiles inside a ring, with rays of green and black tile fanning out across the chequered floor. Beside the bar a low wooden stage holds a microphone stand and a small amp, and a concrete stair with an iron rail climbs the wall behind it to the first floor.
zone.archway.title = the kitchen archway
zone.archway.body = The middle of the café floor, under a stone arch of pale carved mouldings framing the way into the kitchen. The wall around the arch is stripped to red brick, with a chalkboard menu hung beside it and a steel trolley parked against the bricks. Two brick pillars with strip lights stand on the open tiles between the arch and the long tables.
zone.corner.title = the Hardie boys' corner
zone.corner.body = A raised nook at the back of the dining hall, behind a glass partition hung with ivy: a long table with benches under a wall of dark wood panelling, where the dockworkers hold court. A short step and a doorway with a wooden frame lead up into it from the café floor.
zone.backdoor.title = the kitchen back door
zone.backdoor.body = A narrow patch of floor behind the kitchen's back door, boxed in by plaster walls.
zone.kitchen.title = the kitchen
zone.kitchen.body = A galley kitchen behind the arch: a run of white cabinets and a steel sink under a window, a big industrial stove with an extractor hood above it, a green refrigerator against the end wall and a rack of dishes drying over the counter. Shelves of jars and books line the wall by the stove, and a bare bulb hangs from the pipes. A blue door in the back wall leads out.
zone.backroom.title = the back room
zone.backroom.body = A storeroom beyond the bar under two stone arches, its plaster walls flaking to brick. A pinball machine lies tipped on its side among wooden pallets and crates, a standing lamp leans in the corner, and a caged lift shaft with a folding grille stands in the far wall beside the stair to the upper floors.
