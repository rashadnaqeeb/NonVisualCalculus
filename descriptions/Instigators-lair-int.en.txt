zone.pumproom.body = A cavern of brick and iron under the Feld building, its floor of cracked flagstones split by a drop, with big rusted pipes running along the walls and overhead, iron pillars holding the roof, lanterns hung on hooks and a workbench of junk against the bricks. The shore door opens through a wall of reeds at the back.

zone.bunkroom.body = A hollow cut into the channel's side with a bunk bed built of timber, a blanket and pillow on the top bunk, a ladder against it, a table with bowls and a lamp, shelves of jars on the wall and a framed picture pinned to the post.
